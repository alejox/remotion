# Bar: Apple MacBook Pro product page

Reference: https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/ (frames in `design-loop/reference/apple-mbp-*.jpg`).
Translated to video: each mechanism is checked on a filmstrip of rendered frames.

1. **Black is the stage.** Pure black canvas; the graphic occupies a minority of the frame. At least 55% of every frame is empty black. No boxes, panels or borders unless the element IS a UI object (a pill, a button).
2. **Two type sizes per frame, big gap between them.** Headline cap height is at least 2.5x body size. Headlines are 1-2 short lines, sentence case, often ending in a period ("Three chips. Second to none."). Never three competing sizes on one frame.
3. **Emphasis by luminance, not by colour.** Supporting copy is mid-gray; only the key phrase is white. Colour (a gradient or single tint) appears on at most ONE short line per frame, usually the stat.
4. **The number is the payoff.** When a claim has a figure ("Up to 8x faster"), the figure line is the visually loudest thing on screen after the headline, and it arrives last.
5. **One column, one alignment.** Text blocks share a fixed left edge (or a single centered axis) and never mix alignments inside one frame. Generous, consistent side margins (text never within ~8% of a frame edge).
6. **Quiet, one-direction motion.** Elements enter with a fade plus a short rise (small travel, no bounce, no overshoot, no spin), staggered line by line, and settle for longer than they took to arrive. Only one element is moving at any moment.
7. **UI objects are soft and single-accent.** Interactive-looking objects are dark-gray rounded pills with one blue circular affordance. One interaction colour in the whole piece.
