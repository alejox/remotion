# Alejox Gaming edit DNA

Attach `reference/channel-page.png`.

Flat grape/cyan diagonal, slanted white/yellow condensed caps, mostly footage. LOUD MOTION, QUIET DESIGN.

**WEIRD MOVE:** the yellow keyword is 8–12% wider than its card, crosses its edge or the seam, tilted −2° extra. Once per title/section card.

## Moves
- Diagonal split: grape `#5A2885` left, cyan `#25A1DC` right, seam 20° off vertical. Section cards and every wipe.
- Headlines: 2 lines white/yellow, Anton caps, skewX −8°, stroke 4%, hard shadow 4%, blur 0. Display:body ≥ 5:1.
- White disc on seam/corner holds logo or step number.
- Slam in: scale 1.35→1 in 7 frames, one overshoot. Exit 5 frames.
- Flat panels `#111111` 92%. No rims, textures, glows. Max 2 overlays on screen.
- MrBeast rhythm: hard-cut punch-in 1.12x on emphasis words; a sound on every hit; Shorts captions pop 1–3 words; no 4s stretch without a change.

## Bans
- Never a non-condensed headline face, and never lowercase headlines.
- Never a blurred shadow or glow on type.
- Never more than 1 yellow word per frame.
- Never cover the face, and never centre a 16:9 overlay.
- Never emoji, full subtitles in 16:9, glass or blur panels, fade entrances, or particles and flares.
- Never a straight (0° or 90°) brand split.

## Palette and type
- Coverage: footage 72, black 12, grape+cyan 10, white 4, yellow ≤ 3, no magenta.
- Type: Anton for display (fallback Impact), Inter 600/800 for body. At most 3 sizes per frame.

## Archetypes
HOOK, SECTION, LOWER_THIRD, KEYWORD, CALLOUT, COMPARE, OUTRO. Code `src/brand/`.

## Self-check
T1 yellow <5%. T2 ≤3 sizes. T3 display:body ≥5. T4 headline recipe. T5 one breakout. T6 seam 20°±3. T7 safe area. T8 in ≤8f, out ≤5f. T9 text ≥28px@1080. T10 squint = thumbnails. T11 Shorts: no 4s static.
